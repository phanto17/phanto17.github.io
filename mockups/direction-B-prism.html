About me

who · specialisation · interests · goals

I'm a BA Game Development student at Collegium Da Vinci, Poznań (2024 – present), specialising in gameplay programming in Unity 6 and C#. My projects are prototypes I design and code myself: a survivors-like with a data-driven ability system, a deterministic grid-tactics engine, a 2D dungeon crawler.

I care about the psychology of games — telegraphing, tension, the feeling of a "perfect turn". I explore that on my YouTube channel phanto, and it feeds back into how I design mechanics.
