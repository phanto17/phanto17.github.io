About me

Who I am, what I build, where I'm going

I'm a Game Development student at Collegium Da Vinci (2024 – present) specialising in gameplay programming in Unity 6 and C#. I like designing a mechanic, building the system behind it, and tuning it until it reads clearly to the player.

My favourite problems are tactical: deterministic rules, telegraphing, pushing enemies into hazards, a perfect turn. I'm also into the psychology of games — I run the YouTube channel phanto about it.
