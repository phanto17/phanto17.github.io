About me

I'm a Game Development student at Collegium Da Vinci in Poznań, specialising in gameplay programming in Unity and C#. What I enjoy most is the systems layer: data-driven abilities, deterministic turn logic, enemy AI, and the glue that makes a loop replayable.

Outside the engine I think a lot about the psychology of play: tension, telegraphing, risk and reward. That's what my channel phanto is about, and it shapes the design decisions in my prototypes.
